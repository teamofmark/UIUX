3 Inconvenient Truths About Plastic You'll Never Use Once You Know Them
[작성자 부분]
Text: Greenpeace Seoul Office

[문단 - 배경강조]
The uncomfortable truth about the plastic we used and discarded without a second thought continues to be revealed. It is no longer simply an environmental issue, but a plastic problem. Here are three reasons why you absolutely cannot use plastic once you understand it properly, as it leaves you feeling uneasy.

[부제 - 01]
1. Plastic is a 'collection of chemicals'.
    [문단 - 01]
    What is plastic made of? Over 99% of plastic is made from petroleum. Numerous chemical additives are added to color it, make it flexible, or fix it rigidly. And plastic, a mass of countless chemical additives, never decomposes.

    [이미지 - 01]
    [이미지 - 01 설명]
    Greenpeace activists protesting, demanding the reduction of toxic plastics and strong international agreements, starting from the extraction of petroleum raw materials. © Greenpeace / Yejin Kim

    [문단 - 02]
    According to an analysis of 24 recent papers by Greenpeace, it was found that there are approximately 16,000 types of chemicals used or estimated to be present in single-use plastics for convenience foods .

    [문단 - 03]
    Even more shocking is the fact that approximately 4,200 of these are toxic substances harmful to humans and the environment. Furthermore, the majority of the remaining substances are "unknown substances" whose long-term effects on our health have not even been properly identified. In fact, there are as many as 1,396 types of harmful chemicals already found in the human body through food-contact plastics. Plastic containers are, in essence, "walking chemical factories."

 
[부제 - 02]
2. Microplastics that do not decompose but break down and enter our bodies
    [문단 - 01]
    One of the biggest problems with plastic is microplastics. 
    [문단 - 02]
    Plastic does not decompose in nature forever. It merely breaks down into very small, invisible nano-sized particles. When we eat hot delivery food or drink pouch beverages, we end up swallowing these microplastics along with the food.

    [이미지 - 02]
    [이미지 - 02 설명]
    Banner performance claiming that microplastics were found in the brain amidst non-biodegradable plastic waste © Tim Aubry / Greenpeace

    [문단 - 03]
    According to a Greenpeace investigation, the release of microplastics and harmful substances increases exponentially the moment food in plastic packaging is heated. If containers made of materials like polypropylene (PP) are placed in a microwave and heated for just five minutes, as many as 326,000 to 534,000 microplastic particles are released into the food.
    [문단 - 04]
    It is not just delivery food for adults. Microplastics have also been found in the squeeze pouches of baby food that our children eat. A recent investigation by Greenpeace into squeeze pouches from global brands Nestlé and Danone yielded shocking results. 

    [비 순차 목록 - 01]
        Just one teaspoonful: Up to 495 microplastics detected
        An entire pack of baby food pouches contains over 11,000 plastic particles

    [문단 - 05]
    These plastic particles originated from the polyethylene (PE) material used for the pouch lining. Furthermore, some popular yogurt products were found to contain endocrine disruptors (environmental hormones) that can have a fatal impact on children's reproductive systems, growth, and metabolism.  
    [문단 - 06]
    Where do the microplastics that enter our bodies go? Are they simply digested and expelled?
    [문단 - 07]
    Unfortunately, numerous medical studies have already clearly identified microplastics not only in our blood, lungs, and liver, but also deep within the brain and even in the placenta of pregnant women. Research results report that microplastics and harmful chemicals, having penetrated every corner of the body's organs, become embedded between cells and contribute to various modern chronic diseases and immune abnormalities.

 
[부제 - 03]
3. It is not recycled, but thrown away and burned.
    [문단 - 01]
    Did you think that simply using plastic and recycling it would be enough? Only about 9% of the world's plastic production is recycled. Although Korea boasts a high recycling rate, in reality, more than half is incinerated. This is because so-called "thermal recycling," which involves burning plastic waste to generate heat, is cleverly included in Korea's recycling statistics.

    [이미지 - 03]
    [이미지 - 03 설명]
    Single-use plastics and waste being burned instead of recycled © Caner Özkan / Greenpeace
    [문단 - 02]
    More than half of the plastic we diligently separate is not actually recycled; instead, it is essentially burning up with only its outer packaging changed. A more serious problem is that burning plastic, which is essentially lumps of petroleum, releases enormous amounts of greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. The government is shifting the blame for these greenhouse gases onto other industrial sectors, creating a statistical illusion by claiming that it has achieved "waste sector reduction targets." 

    [문단 - 03]
    When you face the inconvenient truth about plastic, it becomes difficult to use this uncomfortable plastic any longer. The solution to the problem of uncomfortable plastic—which constantly pollutes the environment from the moment it is created, does not decompose, and even threatens our health—is to 'not use it.' 

    [문단 - 04]
    To reduce exposure to chemicals and microplastics, we must produce and use less plastic.
