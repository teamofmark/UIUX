A healthy ocean
It is not only a habitat for over 700,000 species of life, but is also necessary for the maintenance of the Earth. The ocean, which provides food and jobs to billions of people, is crucial for the health and well-being of humanity.

However, the ocean is currently facing greater danger than ever before due to plastic waste, destructive fishing, and rising sea surface temperatures. If we work together, we will be able to protect the ocean, countless marine creatures, and people.

Greenpeace activities
Greenpeace calls for the designation of marine protected areas. We are working to protect the ocean by opposing destructive fishing and campaigning to prevent plastic pollution and the climate crisis.

1970 ~ 1989
Opposition to whaling
In 1975, the first whale conservation activity began by blocking Russian whaling ships in rubber boats.

Seal protection
In 1978, the Rainbow Warrior headed to the Orkney Islands in Scotland to stop seal hunting.

Temporary ban on commercial whaling
In 1982, due to strong demands from citizens around the world, the International Whaling Commission declared a temporary ban on commercial whaling.

1990 ~ 2009
Antarctic World Park
As a result of a seven-year campaign, 39 countries signed the Antarctic Treaty and pledged to protect Antarctica from development.

whale sanctuary
In 1994, following Greenpeace's anti-whaling action, the International Whaling Commission decided to ban whaling in Antarctica.

Opposition to the import of nuclear waste
In 1999, I worked in cooperation with domestic environmental groups to prevent Taiwan's nuclear waste from being brought into North Korea.
