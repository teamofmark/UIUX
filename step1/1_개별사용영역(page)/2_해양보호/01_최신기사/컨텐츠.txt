The Global Maritime Treaty, also known as the "BBNJ," has officially entered into force. To commemorate this and call for greater and more effective action, special artworks have appeared on the streets of 15 countries around the world.

[부제 - 02]
> What is the significance of the official entry into force of the 'Benefits of the Global Maritime Treaty (BBNJ)'?
    [문단 - 01]
    The entry into force of this treaty signifies the establishment, for the first time in human history, of a strong legal framework protecting the "high seas," previously abandoned as unclaimed waters. The high seas are vast, encompassing more than half of the Earth's surface, yet the absence of a comprehensive legal framework for their management has left them vulnerable to indiscriminate fishing and destructive development.
    [문단 - 02]
    However, countries that have now ratified the treaty must establish domestic laws and administrative systems for designating marine protected areas (MPAs) on the high seas, conducting environmental impact assessments, and managing genetic resources. They must also participate in international consultations and implementation discussions, including the upcoming Ocean Conference of the Parties (OCP). This is considered the most significant environmental progress humanity has made since the Paris Agreement.
    [문단 - 03]
    The Global Ocean Treaty will go beyond simply protecting the oceans. It will be a crucial turning point, not only slowing the climate crisis and restoring disappearing marine biodiversity, but also ensuring food security for billions of people who depend on the ocean for their livelihoods.

[부제 - 03]
> Why do you emphasize that the '5-year countdown' has started now?
    [문단 - 01]
    Because we have very little time left to fulfill our promise to designate 30% of the world's oceans as protected areas by 2030. Currently, only 0.9% of the high seas are properly protected.
    [문단 - 02]
    To increase this area by 30% in the next five years, we need to establish protected areas faster and more extensively than any conservation effort in human history. This is a colossal challenge, equivalent to creating an area larger than a continent every month.
    [문단 - 03]
    Greenpeace's call for a "five-year countdown" stems from a sense of urgency: if we don't act now, we could miss the golden opportunity to prevent the collapse of the marine ecosystem.
