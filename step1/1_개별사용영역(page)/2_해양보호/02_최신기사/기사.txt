From Busan to the World - 600 Citizens Create Waves of Maritime Treaty
[소제]
Greenpeace Documentary Busan Premiere Completed

[작성자 부분]
Written by: Greenpeace Seoul Office

[색상배경 - 문단]
On April 28th, the Greenpeace Oceans documentary was successfully screened at the Busan Cinema Center Outdoor Theater.

This event is the first large-scale offline citizen participation event held in Korea since Korea ratified the Global Ocean Treaty.

It was an opportunity to send a strong message to governments around the world urging them to swiftly ratify the treaty, in time for the 10th Our Ocean Conference (OOC).

2. Citizen Participation: Confirming the Power of Busan Citizens
[이미지]
[설명]
The Signal premiere was held successfully on April 28, 2025 at the Busan Cinema Center Outdoor Theater. Many audience members are waiting in anticipation to enter.
[문단 - 01]
Following last year's 'Busan Plastic March', Greenpeace Seoul Office went beyond activities focused on the metropolitan area and created a space to listen to citizens' voices in Busan.
[문단 - 02]
Busan citizens stayed until the very end of the screening, and a background explanation of the documentary was provided through a GV (audience dialogue) program held before the screening. It was a place that went beyond a simple film screening and transformed into a place to deliver a profound message of a marine conservation campaign and citizen action .

With over 600 people present and brightening up the event, we could strongly feel the power of citizen participation.
