OOC is an international maritime conference held one month before the United Nations Ocean Assembly held in June, and will be held in Busan until the 30th.
[문단 - 03]
Greenpeace prepared this screening to urge the government representatives of OOC participating countries to promptly ratify the Global Barrier-Free Zone Treaty (BBNJ), the legal foundation for '30X30', and to emphasize that the host country, Korea, can play a more active and leading role in protecting the oceans.
