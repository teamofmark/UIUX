2025 Settlement: A Record of Marine Protection with Citizens
[작성자 부분]
Written by: Greenpeace Seoul Office

[문단 - 배경강조]
    [문단 - 01]
    In 2025, Greenpeace worked tirelessly on the ground around the world to protect the oceans from destruction.
    [문단 - 02]
    The strong support of our citizens led to the ratification of the BBNJ by the Korean government, the first in East Asia, and the achievement of ratification by 60 countries worldwide, marking a historic milestone ushering in a new era of pollution protection.
    [문단 - 03]
    Now, Greenpeace is embarking on a new journey, moving beyond mere ratification to designating a practical marine protected area by 2026.

[순차 목록 - 01 - 부제]
1. Achieved ratification of the global maritime treaty "BBNJ," the first in East Asia

    [이미지 - 01]
    [이미지 - 01 설명]
    Greenpeace and Democratic Party lawmakers Han Jeong-ae and Kim Young-bae held a National Assembly screening of the marine documentary Signal on March 4 and urged the Korean government to ratify the global marine treaty.

    [문단 - 01]
    In the midst of the climate crisis, the ocean protects us by absorbing accumulated heat and carbon in the atmosphere. However, only 2% of the high seas are designated as protected areas, leaving them vulnerable to indiscriminate development and overfishing. The solution to this problem is the Global Marine Treaty, also known as the BBNJ.
        [참조]
        * BBNJ Agreement: 
        Biological Diversity Beyond Areas of National Jurisdiction

    [문단 - 02]
    Greenpeace has been working urgently to ensure the South Korean government ratifies the treaty by the third United Nations Ocean Conference (UNOC) in June 2025. In February, Greenpeace held a joint press conference with civil society at the National Assembly to emphasize the urgency of ratification, and through a series of meetings with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and relevant lawmakers' offices, Greenpeace has strongly argued the need to expand high seas protection zones.

    [이미지 - 02]
    [이미지 - 02 설명]
    The flags of the 60 BBNJ signatories form the number 60.

    [문단 - 03]
    This all-out pressure and the voices of the people brought about real change. In March 2025, the South Korean government finally ratified the BBNJ, becoming the first country in East Asia and the 21st UN member state.

    [문단 - 04]
    Korea's proactive participation has created a huge wave in the international community, leading to the remarkable achievement of ratification by 60 countries worldwide last September. The global maritime treaty, scheduled to officially enter into force in January 2026, will now serve as a powerful and practical shield to protect our oceans from destruction.

[순차 목록 - 02 - 부제]
2. Ocean Documentary ' SEAGNAL' : A Signal from the Sea Sent by 3,000 Voices
About 600 citizens are waiting to watch the signal.

    [이미지 - 03]
    [이미지 - 03 설명]
    To build on the momentum of the 10th Our Ocean Conference (OOC), approximately 600 citizens attended a screening of an ocean documentary held at the Busan Cinema Center Outdoor Theater on April 28, 2025. © Greenpeace

    [문단 - 01]
    Over 3,000 supporters have joined forces to raise awareness of the invisible ocean crisis. Produced through the voluntary participation of citizens, the documentary "SEAGNAL: The Last Signal of the Sea" has become a powerful campaign voice, bringing the cries of the ocean, hidden beneath the surface, to the world.

    [문단 - 02]
    "SEAGNAL" wasn't just a simple marine documentary; it served as a catalyst for real policy change. The National Assembly screening held in March, ahead of the ratification bill's passage, served as a crucial turning point, directly conveying citizens' earnest messages to the voting members of the National Assembly. In the subsequent plenary session, the BBNJ ratification bill achieved the valuable feat of unanimous approval by all members present.

    [문단 - 03]
    The desire for ocean protection reached a peak in Busan last April, when the 10th Our Ocean Conference (OOC) was held. At a documentary screening held on the conference's opening day, over 600 citizens witnessed the ocean's final warning and urged world government representatives to take immediate and substantive action.

    [이미지 - 04]
    [이미지 - 04 설명]
    Photo credit: Korean Federation for Environmental Movement website. [Press Release] Korean Civil Society Unveils 'Demands for the Ocean' at the 10th Our Ocean Conference.

    [문단 - 04]
    Furthermore, approximately 20 civil society organizations, including Greenpeace, held a joint press conference at the OOC conference site and declared a strong solidarity for ocean protection. The subsequent nationwide documentary screening was a valuable journey that not only spread the news of Korea's ratification of the BBNJ but also demonstrated how the voices of individual citizens can change the map of the ocean.

[순차 목록 - 03 - 부제]
3. International solidarity to protect the deep-sea ecosystem
 
    [이미지 - 05]
    [이미지 - 05 설명]
    Greenpeace Germany activists stage a peaceful protest in front of a giant pink octopus balloon measuring 8x10x5 meters to support deep sea protection in front of the Bundestag on the banks of the Spree River. © Anne Barth / Greenpeace

    [문단 - 01]
    The International Seabed Authority (ISA) General Assembly held in Jamaica last July was a critical turning point that would determine the fate of the deep-sea ecosystem. Greenpeace issued a statement at the event, strongly urging member states, including the Korean government, to adopt a firm moratorium on deep-sea mining.

    [문단 - 02]
    To raise awareness of the vulnerability of the deep sea, which takes hundreds of years to recover once destroyed, Greenpeace launched a comprehensive digital campaign highlighting the value of deep-sea ecosystems and warned of the catastrophic future posed by deep-sea mining. Our call for ecosystem conservation to take precedence over short-term corporate profits is creating a massive movement to stop deep-sea mining. Against the greed that views the deep sea as a mere storehouse of resources, Greenpeace will continue its fight to protect the deepest reaches of the ocean.

[순차 목록 - 04 - 부제]
4. [2026 Preview] Beyond ‘Ratification’ to the Implementation of ‘Protected Area Designation’

    [이미지 - 06]
    [이미지 - 06 설명]
    Freediver Amber Bourke swims with bottlenose dolphins off Lord Howe Island. © Greenpeace / BRIDGET FERGUSON

    [문단 - 01]
    If 2025 was the "year of preparation" that led to the ratification of the Global Ocean Treaty, 2026 will be the "year of implementation" to put that promise into practice. With the treaty officially entering into force in January 2026, Greenpeace's efforts will become more concrete and intense.

    [문단 - 02]
    Greenpeace plans to launch a campaign in 2026 to urge the South Korean government to officially announce its plan to designate Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) in high-priority waters, including the Emperor's Sea, or to begin preparing proposals. In particular, ahead of the Fourth United Nations Ocean Conference (UNOC4), scheduled to be held in South Korea in 2028, Greenpeace will launch a roadmap over the next three years to raise awareness of the government's responsibilities alongside citizens.

    [문단 - 03]
    Our voices will not cease until the Korean government, befitting its position as the host nation, effectively implements the expansion of high-seas protection zones and plays a pioneering role in restoring the oceans from destruction.

All these changes were possible thanks to your support, and these valuable results are the result of your cooperation.
