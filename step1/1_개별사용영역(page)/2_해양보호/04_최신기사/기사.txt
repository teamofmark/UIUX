Global promise to protect the ocean - 2025 Our Ocean Conference (OOC) held in Busan

[작성자 부분]
Written by: Greenpeace Seoul Office

[색상배경 - 문단]
The 10th Our Ocean Conference (OOC) will be held in Busan from April 28th to 30th.

OOC is a high-level international conference that has been discussing global marine issues such as marine protected areas, marine pollution, climate change, and maritime security since 2014.

[부제 - 01]
Our Ocean Conference (OOC) will be held in Busan in April.

[설명]
The 10th Our Ocean Conference (OOC) will be held in Busan from April 28th to 30th.
[문단 - 01]
The 10th meeting to be held in Busan this year will discuss six basic agendas and special agendas, including marine protected areas, marine economy, climate change, marine pollution, maritime security, and sustainable fisheries, as well as the digital ocean.
[문단 - 02]
In particular, the international community is currently promoting the '30x30' initiative to designate 30% of the high seas as marine protected areas. However, the current high seas protected areas are only about 2% worldwide, and Korea's marine protected area designation rate is even lower at 1.8%.
[문단 - 03]
To achieve the 30X30 goal, the Global Barrier-Free Zone Treaty (BBNJ) needs to enter into force as soon as possible. This requires ratification by at least 60 countries before it can enter into force.
[문단 - 04]
Korea, the host country of this year's OOC, became the first East Asian country to ratify the treaty for its entry into force in March.
[문단 - 05]
As 21 countries have ratified it so far, more attention from the international community is needed.
