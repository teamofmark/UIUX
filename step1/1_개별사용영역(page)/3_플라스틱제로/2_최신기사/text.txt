'Zero Plastic Voyage' - A Special Journey of the 'Rainbow Warrior' with Greenpeace Supporters

[작성자 부분]
Written by: David Lim

[문단 - 배경강조]
Greenpeace's environmental exploration ship 'Rainbow Warrior' visited Korea at a historic moment to create a new future without pollution, and some very special guests came to visit. On November 15th and 23rd, Greenpeace had a special time with our precious Korean supporters on board the Rainbow Warrior as part of its 'Zero Plastic Voyage'.

[이미지설명]
Rainbow Warrior conducting surveys on deep-sea fishing fleets in the North Pacific and the Emperor Seamount

[부제목]
Greenpeace's environmental exploration vessel protecting the Earth on the front lines

[문단 - 1]
Greenpeace has environmental monitoring ships that travel around the world to discover, record, and report environmental problems. They investigate marine ecosystems with scientists and take amazing action at sites of ocean destruction. They also meet with citizens around the world to inform them of environmental issues.

[문단 - 2]
Greenpeace has three environmental monitoring vessels operating in Korea, including the Rainbow Warrior, which recently visited Korea, the Arctic Sunrise, and the Witness.

[이미지설명]
Greenpeace Plastics Campaigner Kim Nara (Seoul Office) and Rainbow Warrior Captain Hetti Gynen stood in front of the Rainbow Warrior in Busan on November 15 to urge governments to support a strong international plastics agreement that includes production cuts ahead of the INC5 international plastics agreement.

[부제목]
Why the Rainbow Warrior Came to Korea

[문단 - 3]
The Rainbow Warrior arrived in Busan Port on November 15 after passing through Hong Kong and Taiwan. This voyage is to urge world leaders attending the 5th International Plastics Convention (INC5) in Busan to come up with a concrete and strong agreement to reduce plastic production. This is the first time in eight years that the Rainbow Warrior has visited Korea.

[문단 - 4]
This INC5 meeting is an opportunity to create a new future without plastic pollution, making Rainbow Warrior’s visit to Korea more meaningful than ever.

A meaningful time with Greenpeace supporters
Greenpeace invited Greenpeace supporters to the 'Rainbow Warrior' and had a very meaningful time.

[문단 - 6]
We held two events on November 15th in Busan and 23rd in Incheon, where sponsors were invited to tour the Rainbow Warrior and learn about the environmental monitoring ship's activities.

[문단 - 7]
Astrid Huber Loibl, Finance Director for Greenpeace East Asia, warmly welcomed the donors to the event and expressed her gratitude and welcome for joining Greenpeace’s vision.

[이미지설명]

[문단 - 8]
Kim Na-ra, Plastic Campaigner, and Kim Mi-kyung, Greenpeace East Asia Project Manager, introduced the plastic campaign activities and shared why Greenpeace is serious about the 'International Plastics Convention (INC5)' and informed supporters about the importance of the convention.
