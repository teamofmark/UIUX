[문단배너 - 01]
Campaign Goals
Plastic pollution is a growing crisis. We must eradicate plastic pollution by concluding a legally binding international agreement on plastics, reducing plastic production, and transitioning to a system based on reuse and refilling.

[문단 사진 구성]
Did you know?
Microplastics are found everywhere: in Antarctic snow, in wild animal feces, and even in our blood and breast milk. Plastics are overwhelming our lives. Yet, the global recycling rate remains at a mere 9%. Plastics, more than 99% made from fossil fuels, emit greenhouse gases even after they are produced and discarded, contributing to the climate crisis.

[문단배너 - 02]
Problem
Single-use plastics, which account for 40% of total plastic production, are largely replaceable and unnecessary. The "disposable" culture, driven by convenience, leads us to produce, consume, and discard a significant amount of plastic.
