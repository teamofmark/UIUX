Your first donation will be processed immediately upon completion of your sponsorship. Regular donation payments are due on the 10th of each month. If payment is not received, repayments will be made on the 15th, 20th, and 27th of the same month.

For automatic transfers, if the payment date falls on a public holiday or weekend, payment will be processed on the following business day. (*If the Seoul office holiday falls on the day before the payment date, payment may be delayed by one day.)

For credit card payments, payments are processed on the due date, regardless of holidays. However, payments may be processed on the next business day during system processing.
