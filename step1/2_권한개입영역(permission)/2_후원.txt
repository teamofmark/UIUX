[부제 - 06]
Greenpeace Sponsorship FAQs

[컴포넌트 - 아코디언 / accComponent]
Q1. When will the first donation be paid after the sponsorship is completed?
    A.
    Your first donation will be processed immediately upon completion of your sponsorship. Regular donation payments are due on the 10th of each month. If payment is not received, repayments will be made on the 15th, 20th, and 27th of the same month.

Q2. What happens if the payment date falls on a public holiday or weekend?
    A.
    [비 순차 목록 - 01]
    If your automatic transfer falls on a public holiday or weekend, payment will be processed on the next business day. (*If the day before the payment date falls on a Seoul office holiday, payment may be delayed by one day.)
    [비 순차 목록 - 02]
    Credit card payments are processed on the payment date, regardless of whether it falls on a holiday or not, but may be delayed to the next business day during system processing.

Q3. Can minors also sponsor?
    A.
    Sponsors under the age of 14 may make donations with the consent of their legal representative. Minors between the ages of 14 and 19 may only make regular donations of up to 30,000 won and one-time donations of up to 300,000 won.

Q4. If I make a donation, can I receive a year-end tax deduction?
    A.
    You can receive year-end tax deduction benefits. After signing up, please be sure to enter your personal information for year-end tax settlement. To maintain its independence, Greenpeace does not accept government or corporate sponsorship.

Q5. How do I inquire about sponsorship?
    A.
    For other inquiries, please refer to the FAQ. You can also contact the Sponsorship Management Team by email at help.kr@greenpeace.org or by phone at 02-3144-1997 (Weekdays, 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
