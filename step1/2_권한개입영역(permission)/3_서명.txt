#1. Dying white coral

Rising global temperatures are significantly impacting the ocean, the planet's largest carbon sink. Once vibrant and vibrant, corals are losing their vibrant colors and turning white due to rising water temperatures. Coral death not only disrupts marine habitats but can also lead to the collapse of the Earth's food chain and ecosystems.

Did you know that in addition to plastic, plastic waste, and chemicals, noise also pollutes the ocean? The engine noise and sound waves from large ships disrupt the communication and feeding habits of marine animals like sperm whales, threatening their survival.

#3. The deep sea, an unknown world at risk of being discovered 
[이미지 - 03]
The deep sea remains a largely unknown world to humanity, to the point where it's said we know more about the far side of the moon than the depths of the ocean. Massive mining equipment deployed in the deep sea will excavate the seabed, causing irreparable damage to the deep-sea ecosystem for hundreds of years. The deep sea is not a resource for exploitation; it's a last bastion, a vital necessity for human survival.

In September 2025, 60 countries around the world ratified the Global Marine Protection Treaty (BBNJ), which will officially enter into force in 120 days.
