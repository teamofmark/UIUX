Having been ratified by 60 countries, the Global Marine Protection Treaty (BBNJ) is now 120 days away from entering into force. The first meeting of the Conference of the Parties to the Convention, held after its entry into force, will mark the beginning of the international decision-making process for designating marine protected areas on the high seas.
