    In September 2025, 60 countries worldwide ratified the Global Ocean Treaty (BBNJ), which is set to officially enter into force in 120 days. This marks a historic milestone toward the international community's commitment (30x30) to designate 30% of the high seas as marine protected areas by 2030. Furthermore, the Seoul office produced the documentary film *Signal* with approximately 3,000 citizens, leading to its theatrical release and raising awareness of the importance of ocean protection. Now, to urge governments around the world to take action to expand marine protected areas in the high seas and halt deep-sea mining, Greenpeace, together with citizens, demands the following:
