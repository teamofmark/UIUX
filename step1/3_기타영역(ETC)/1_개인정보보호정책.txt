Privacy Policy
[색상배경 - 문단]
[문단 - 01]
(Revised on May 2, 2023)
The Greenpeace Foundation (hereinafter referred to as the “Foundation”) establishes and discloses the following personal information processing guidelines in order to protect the personal information of data subjects and to promptly and smoothly process complaints related thereto in accordance with Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act.
[문단 - 02]
The Foundation considers users' personal information important and complies with the personal information protection regulations of laws and regulations related to personal information that information and communication service providers must comply with, such as the "Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection", the "Personal Information Protection Act", the "Telecommunications Business Act", and the "Communications Secrets Protection Act", as well as the personal information protection guidelines established by the Korea Communications Commission.
The Foundation informs users of how and for what purpose the personal information they provide is used and what measures are taken to protect personal information through the personal information handling policy disclosed on the website.
This personal information handling policy was implemented on February 1, 2014, and was revised on February 13, 2018, and September 15, 2021. The contents may be changed due to revisions or amendments to government laws and guidelines or to provide better services, so please check the contents frequently when visiting the site. If the Foundation revises the personal information handling policy, it will be notified through a website notice (or individual notice).

Article 7 Matters concerning personal information management (viewing, correction, deletion, etc.)
Users and their legal representatives may view or correct their own registered personal information or that of their children under the age of 14 at any time, and may also request cancellation or deletion of their membership.
In order to view or correct personal information of a user or a child under the age of 14, click on “User Information Inquiry” and “Modify Basic Information” and go through the identity verification process to directly view, correct or withdraw your information. In addition, if you contact the Personal Information Manager in writing, by phone or by email, we will take action without delay.
If a user requests correction of errors in personal information, we will take action without delay. In addition, if incorrect personal information has already been provided to a third party, we will promptly notify the third party of the results of the correction process to ensure that the correction is made.
Such rights may be exercised through a legal representative of a child under the age of 14 or a proxy, such as a person authorized by the data subject. In this case, a power of attorney in the format of Appendix 11 of the Enforcement Regulations of the Personal Information Protection Act must be submitted.
The Foundation processes personal information that has been terminated or deleted at the request of the user or his/her legal representative in accordance with the provisions of “Greenpeace Foundation’s Retention and Use Period for Personal Information Collected” and processes it so that it cannot be viewed or used for any other purpose.
Requests to view, correct, cancel or delete personal information may be restricted according to relevant laws and regulations. In such cases, the Foundation will notify you within 10 days of receiving the request through the Personal Information Manager.
Article 8 Matters concerning the installation, operation and refusal of automatic personal information collection devices
The Foundation uses 'cookies' to store and retrieve user information from time to time. Cookies are small pieces of information that the server used to operate the Foundation's website sends to your browser and may be stored on the user's computer hard disk. The Foundation uses cookies for the following purposes.

 Purpose of use:
To analyze the frequency of access and visit times of members and non-members, identify users' tastes and areas of interest, track their footprints, and determine the extent of participation in various events and the number of visits.
 Cookie Installation/Operation and Refusal
Users have the option to choose whether to install cookies. Therefore, by setting options in the web browser, you can allow all cookies, confirm each time a cookie is saved, or refuse to save all cookies.
Article 9 Matters concerning measures to ensure the safety of personal information
The Foundation is taking the following measures to ensure the security of personal information.

Administrative measures:  Establishment and implementation of internal management plan, regular personal information protection education, etc.
Technical measures:  Management of access rights to personal information processing systems, etc., control of unauthorized access from outside through security mail, personal certificates, and specific programs, installation of in-house PC security program (ESET) that encrypts unique identification information, etc.
Physical measures:  Access control (access rights management) and installation of locking devices in computer rooms, archives, etc.
