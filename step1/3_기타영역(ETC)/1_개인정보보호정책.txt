privacy policy
[문단 - 01]
(Revised on July 4, 2025) Greenpeace Foundation (hereinafter referred to as the “Foundation”) establishes and discloses the following personal information processing policy in accordance with Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act to protect the personal information of data subjects and to promptly and smoothly process complaints related thereto.

[문단 - 02]
The Foundation considers users' personal information important and complies with the personal information protection regulations of laws and regulations related to personal information that information and communication service providers must comply with, such as the "Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection," the "Personal Information Protection Act," the "Telecommunications Business Act," and the "Communications Secrets Protection Act," as well as the personal information protection guidelines established by the Korea Communications Commission. The Foundation informs users of how and for what purpose the personal information they provide is used and what measures are taken to protect personal information through the personal information processing policy disclosed on the website. The history of this personal information processing policy can be checked here , and the contents may be changed due to revisions to government laws and guidelines or to provide better services, so please check the contents frequently when visiting the site. If the Foundation revises the personal information processing policy, it will be announced through a website notice (or individual notice).

Article 5 Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties
In principle, the Foundation does not provide users' personal information to external parties. The Foundation processes personal information only within the scope specified in Article 1 (Purpose of Personal Information Processing). Personal information is only provided to third parties when required by Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act, including with the consent of the data subject or in accordance with special legal provisions.
The Foundation provides personal information to third parties in the following cases.
If users have given prior consent at the request of the Foundation
When there is a request from an investigative agency in accordance with the provisions of the law or in accordance with the procedures and methods stipulated by the law for investigative purposes.
Rules on the Establishment and Supervision of Non-Profit Corporations under the Ministry of Public Administration and Security and its Subordinate Agencies (Article 8 of the same Act) In cases where it is unavoidable to inspect and supervise the affairs of non-profit corporations pursuant to Article 37 of the Civil Act, the competent authority may order the submission of related documents, ledgers, or other reference materials, or inspect the status of affairs and assets.
Documents related to settlement of accounts and reports on assets contributed to public interest corporations (Article 41 of the Enforcement Decree of the Gift Tax Act)
Obligation to undergo external accounting audit (Article 50 of the Inheritance Tax Act, Article 3 of the Act on External Audit of Stock Companies)
Obligation to disclose financial statements (Article 50 of the Inheritance Tax Act)
Obligation to prepare, store, and submit donation receipt issuance details (Article 112 of the Corporate Tax Act)

Article 8 Matters concerning personal information management (viewing, correction, deletion, etc.)
Users and their legal representatives may view or correct their own registered personal information or that of children under the age of 14 at any time, and may request cancellation of membership or deletion of personal information.
To view or correct personal information of users or children under the age of 14, click "User Information Inquiry" or "Modify Basic Information." To cancel your membership (withdraw consent), click "Request Membership Withdrawal." After completing the identity verification process, you can directly view, correct, or cancel your membership. Alternatively, contact the Personal Information Protection Officer in writing, by phone, or by email, and we will take action without delay.
If a user requests correction of errors in their personal information, we will take action without delay. Furthermore, if incorrect personal information has already been provided to a third party, we will promptly notify the third party of the results of the correction process to ensure the correction is made.
These rights may be exercised through a legal representative of a child under the age of 14 or a proxy authorized by the data subject. In such cases, a power of attorney in the format of Appendix 11 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act must be submitted.
Personal information that has been canceled or deleted at the request of the user or his/her legal representative is processed in accordance with Article 3 and is processed so that it cannot be viewed or used for any other purpose.
Requests to view, correct, cancel, or delete personal information may be restricted by relevant laws and regulations. In such cases, the Foundation will send a notification within 10 days of receiving the request through the Personal Information Manager.
Article 9. Matters concerning the installation, operation, and refusal of automatic personal information collection devices.
The Foundation uses "cookies" to store and retrieve user information. Cookies are small pieces of information sent to your browser by the server that operates the Foundation's website and may also be stored on your computer's hard drive. The Foundation uses cookies for the following purposes:

Purpose of use: To analyze the frequency of access and visit times of members and non-members, to identify users' preferences and areas of interest, to track their footprints, and to determine the extent of participation in various events and the number of visits.
Users have the option to choose whether to install cookies . Therefore, by setting options in their web browser, they can allow all cookies, request confirmation each time a cookie is saved, or reject all cookies.
Article 10 Matters concerning measures to ensure the security of personal information
The Foundation is taking the following measures to ensure the security of personal information.

Administrative measures: Establishment and implementation of internal management plans, regular personal information protection training, etc.
Technical measures: Access rights management for personal information processing systems, etc.; control of unauthorized access from outside through secure mail, personal certificates, and specific programs; encryption of unique identification information, etc.; installation of in-house PC security programs (ESET).
Physical measures: Access control (access rights management) and installation of locking devices in computer rooms, archives, etc.
